**Not assigned from memory or general knowledge.**

## 9. Causality
**WHO-UMC: Probable/Likely (provisional).**

Support:
- appropriate temporal relationship,
- improvement following withdrawal,
- clinically plausible reaction.

Limitations:
- alternative causes incompletely assessed,
- no detailed examination,
- no exact dates/dose,
- no objective diagnostic evidence,
- no rechallenge.

## 10. Narrative
A 45-year-old female received amoxicillin for a suspected bacterial infection. Approximately three days after initiation, she developed generalized skin rash and itching. The suspected medicine was discontinued, following which the reported symptoms improved. Rechallenge was not performed. Based on the available information, a temporal relationship exists between amoxicillin exposure and the reported reaction. The case requires follow-up for exact treatment dates, dose/route, concomitant medicines, allergy history, clinical description, treatment, outcome and reporter details. The event is provisionally assessed as non-serious and the causal relationship as probable/likely.

## 11. Follow-up plan
Request:
1. Exact patient age/date of birth or coded identifier.
2. Reporter name/qualification/contact.
3. Exact amoxicillin product, dose, route and frequency.
4. Start and stop dates.
5. Exact onset date/time.
6. Full rash description and diagnosis.
7. Allergy history.
8. All concomitant medicines and recent exposures.
9. Treatment for the reaction.
10. Outcome and recovery date.
11. Hospitalization/emergency treatment.
12. Any recurrence after exposure.
13. Relevant laboratory/clinical findings.
14. Product batch/lot where available.

## 12. Final case-processing decision
**Case status:** Initial educational ICSR simulation — incomplete for real-world finalization.

**Causality:** Probable/Likely (provisional).

**Seriousness:** Non-serious on supplied facts.

**Expectedness:** Pending comparison with the applicable current reference safety information.

**Follow-up:** Required.
